Short Channel Effect Optimization with High-k Gate Dielectrics High-k 물질 적용을 통한 소자의 SCE 최적화

Compared SiO2, Al2O3 and HfO2 as gate dielectrics at a fixed physical thickness of 5 nm. Because a higher dielectric constant increases gate capacitance at the same thickness, the transfer characteristics shift and the gate gains stronger electrostatic control over the channel.

  • ToolSentaurus TCAD (Process / Device / Visual)
  • DielectricsSiO2 · Al2O3 · HfO2
  • Thickness5 nm (physical, fixed)
  • BiasVd = 1.0 V, Vg sweep 0–1 V
  • Sentaurus Process
  • Sentaurus Device
