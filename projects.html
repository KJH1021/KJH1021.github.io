Semiconductor Value Chain Financial Analysis 반도체 밸류체인 재무 분석 도구

A Python pipeline that pulls financial statements and annual business reports directly from Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, normalizes XBRL account codes so that companies reporting under different account names can be compared on the same axis, and tracks year-over-year changes in the narrative sections of business reports.

  • TypeIndependent project
  • Scope12 companies × 5 years (2021–2025)
  • SourceDART electronic disclosure, Financial Supervisory Service
  • HypothesisCapex cuts at IDMs would reach materials suppliers first, then equipment vendors, with a 6-12 month lag — rejected by the data
  • ResultAll three tiers bottomed in the same year (2023). What differed was amplitude, not timing: equipment revenue swung 1.6× the IDMs while a captive vendor moved ±3%
  • Python
  • pandas
  • lxml
  • OpenDART API
  • GitHub Pages
